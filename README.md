> The river doesn't want to end; it wants to become bigger and deeper.

My primary academic and career focus lies in **Markov Decision Processes (MDPs)** and **Reinforcement Learning (RL)**—studying sequential decision-making, dynamic programming, and multi-agent dynamics from their mathematical foundations. In parallel, I conduct funded undergraduate research in **Software Engineering** applied to **serious games for clinical and cognitive treatment**, alongside building low-level simulation and game systems in C++.

---

### 🔬 Research & Academia

- **Parallel Research (`MDPs` & `Reinforcement Learning` — Career Focus):** Investigating formal decision-making models, exact dynamic programming (*Value Iteration*), *Deep Q-Networks (DQN)*, and *Markov Games* across stochastic and simultaneous-action environments without black-box wrappers.
- **Undergraduate Research (`FAPESP` — University of São Paulo):** Research in **Software Engineering** focused on the architectural design, modeling, and development of **serious games for therapeutic and cognitive treatment**, integrating structured telemetry pipelines for player-state evaluation.
- **Teaching Assistantships (`PEEG/USP` & `DASI`):**
  - *Scholarship TA:* **Introduction to Statistics (ACH2053)** and **Matrices, Vectors, and Analytic Geometry (ACH2033)**.
  - *Voluntary TA:* **Calculus I (ACH2011)** and the introductory C++ curriculum (**Sapphiron**) at Conway-USP.
- **Technical Talks & Outreach:** Co-presenter of *"Que Haja Luz! Descobrindo a Pipeline de Renderização Gráfica"* (on the graphics rendering pipeline) and support organizer of *"PS2: Uma Retrospectiva"* (on PlayStation 2 hardware architecture).
