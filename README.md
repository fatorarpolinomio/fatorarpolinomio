My primary academic and career focus lies in **Markov Decision Processes (MDPs)** and **Reinforcement Learning (RL)**, studying sequential decision-making, dynamic programming, and multi-agent dynamics from their mathematical foundations. In parallel, I conduct funded undergraduate research in **Software Engineering** applied to **serious games for clinical and cognitive treatment**, alongside building low-level simulation and game systems in C and C++.
